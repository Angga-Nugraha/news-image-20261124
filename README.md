One image summing up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026, as a
newspaper front page. The masthead names the date; the hero story is the
burning oil tankers in the Strait of Hormuz — Iran's unverified claim of
three tankers hit by mines despite a US escort. Below it, four vignettes:
Grok sued by Virginia and Oregon, Sanofi's $41B deal to buy Insmed, NATO's
vow to defend space, and the magnitude 5.6 earthquake in eastern Nepal.
All labels are rendered crisply as text, and every story is shown as
reported — the Hormuz claim is labelled unverified.

Competition: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124

`main.py` (Python standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` and verifies it with a sha256 check.
